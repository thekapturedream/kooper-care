THE ARCHITECTURE

Three layers. One product.

Each app does one job exceptionally well. The Context Switcher routes you between them. The Care Graph keeps every change in sync, instantly, across every app, every stakeholder.

LAYER 1

App Layer

20 focused micro-apps. Each one purpose-built. Same brand chrome, same auth, but a workspace tuned to its job. Recording is one app — the AI handles every domain. A carer doing wound dressing opens kooper · charts, not ten different recording tools.

20 apps Role-loaded Mobile-native
LAYER 2

Context Switcher

The connective tissue. Cmd+K command bar searches every app. Tap a resident anywhere — switcher routes you to the most relevant app for your role and their current state. Mid-task hand-offs feel like one product.

Cmd+K search Smart hand-offs Auto-switching
LAYER 3

Care Graph

The data spine. One Postgres source of truth. Every entity, every relationship, every change versioned. Every event published to subscribers in real time. Field-level RBAC. Immutable audit trail. One write — every app updates instantly.

Realtime sync Field-level RBAC Immutable audit
The kicker: change a care plan in kooper · care plans → the next note in kooper · recording already knows. Update a med in kooper · mar → kooper · vitals flags the affected NEWS2 thresholds in real time. Family member messages from kooper · family → the assigned carer's kooper · recording shows the message at the top of their next shift.
