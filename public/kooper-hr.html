        Account type

        The account type sets the apps that default-on, the billing model, the support tier, and the contractual terms. Switch types from here — most operators start on Single home and graduate as they grow.

        CURRENT SELECTED

        Single home

        One CQC-registered location. The default starting point for first-time digital adopters.

        • • 1 community · up to 60 residents
        • • Apps default-on: hr · care plans · recording · charts · mar
        • • Billing: per user per app, monthly, no commitment
        • • Support: business hours, email + chat
        UPGRADE

        Care home group

        Two or more CQC-registered locations under common ownership. Multi-site rollup activates.

        • • 2-30 communities
        • • Multi-site app activates · group rollup reports
        • • Billing: per user per app + £0.20/resident/mo group fee
        • • Support: priority email + named CS contact

        Supported living

        Tenants in their own homes with care visits. Different consent model, different rota patterns.

        • • 1+ supported-living scheme
        • • Consent model: tenant-led; family read-only by default
        • • Apps default-on: hr · recording · charts · risk · activities
        • • Billing: per user per app, no per-resident fee

        Domiciliary care

        Staff visiting people in their own homes. Geo-routing on the rota; per-visit billing pulls from recording events.

        • • Visit-based rota with travel time
        • • Apps default-on: hr · recording · charts · risk
        • • Billing: per visit · LA-friendly invoice exports
        • • Mobile-first: kooper · recording on iPad/phone
        ENTERPRISE

        NHS block contract

        NHS Trust commissioning multiple providers under one contract. Block billing, GP-Connect required, dedicated CSM.

        • • Block billing · ICB-compatible PO numbers
        • • GP-Connect required · FHIR R4
        • • Dedicated Customer Success Manager
        • • 99.95% uptime SLA · UK data sovereignty guarantee

        Solo / sole trader

        Independent nurse, agency owner, or care consultant. One worker, multiple clients, simple billing.

        • • 1 worker · multi-client
        • • Apps default-on: hr · recording · charts
        • • Billing: flat £19/mo · all base apps
        • • Self-serve, community support

        • • 30 days · pre-loaded mock community
        • • Every app available, sandbox-flagged
        • • No credit card · no auto-conversion
        • • Convert to any production account type after
        How worker types affect billing

        Each worker on the platform pays per-user-per-app — but the worker type changes the payroll export and the rota constraints, not the app price.

        Full-time / Part-time
        PAYE · per-user-per-app · max 48h/week WTR enforced.
        Agency
        Invoice routed to agency · DBS revalidated each engagement · agency app fee added.
        Contractor
        CIS-compatible export · UTR on file · WTR not enforced.
        Volunteer
        No payroll · DBS required · max-hours soft cap · counts toward CQC SAF Caring evidence.
        Bank
        Float-pool first call when sickness fires · pay rate variable · payroll on shifts worked.
        Family / informal
        Support workers who are also family · separate consent model · no recording-event RBAC inheritance.
